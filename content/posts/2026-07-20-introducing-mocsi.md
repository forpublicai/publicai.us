The Maine-based data center initiative has a new name: **MOCSI**, the Maine Open Compute Services Initiative.

MOCSI is community-governed open compute in Maine—local ownership of the physical asset, an explicit public-services carveout, and open, auditable AI models. It is the infrastructure behind every service on [publicai.us](/): chat, permitting help, library kiosks, and the community agents we are building with Maine organizations.

We renamed the initiative to make the connection clearer: MOCSI is not a distant hyperscaler warehouse. It is Maine infrastructure built to serve Mainers, with a defined governance structure and a 12–18 month evaluate/scale/sunset pilot period.

**What this means for you:**

- If you want to **use AI services**, start with our [services page](/#services)—you do not need to understand the data center to get value today.
- If you want to **support community-owned compute**, learn more on our [MOCSI page](/mocsi/) and reach out about funding or partnership.

Maine can lead on a replicable model: concrete public services running on infrastructure communities actually own.
