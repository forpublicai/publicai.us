Opening or expanding a small business in Maine often means navigating a maze of permits, licenses, and local regulations—often across multiple agencies with different timelines and requirements.

Today we are launching the **Permitting assistant**, a Public AI service that helps Maine small business owners understand what they may need before they apply.

## What it does

- Identify permits required for your business type and location
- Understand timelines, fees, and application steps
- Clarify zoning, health, safety, and environmental requirements
- Prepare checklists before visiting town offices or filing online

The assistant uses a conversational interface—the same Public AI platform as our general chat, running on [MOCSI](/mocsi/) infrastructure in Maine.

## Try it

Visit the [Permitting assistant page](/permitting/) or start directly at [chat.publicai.co](https://chat.publicai.co).

This tool provides guidance, not legal advice. Always confirm requirements with your local municipality or a qualified professional.

## More services coming
